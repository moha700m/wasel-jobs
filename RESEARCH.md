# Saudi hiring by email: research notes (Oct 2026)

## How it works
- Many Saudi employers, especially private sector, contracting, O&M, healthcare and retail, post vacancies on LinkedIn, X, news sites (Sabq, Slaati, Okaz) and job blogs (alwzifa, wathafni, rwad.net, dmc-education) ending with «ارسل سيرتك الذاتية على الإيميل» / "Send your CV to …".
- Large employers mostly use portals (Jadarat, the national HRSD/HRDF portal, Taqat legacy, Bayt, LinkedIn Easy Apply, GulfTalent, Naukrigulf). Email is the common path for SMEs and contractors.
- The subject line is used for sorting, often automatically. Common required formats:
  - `Job Title + Full Name` (FAOMCO: "Please mention Job Title + your Full Name in the email subject")
  - `Position Title – Candidate Name` (Yamas)
  - `المسمى الوظيفي – المدينة` (SACO: «كتابة مسمى الوظيفة والمدينة في عنوان الرسالة»)
  - With a reference: `أخصائي مشتريات، إعلان SC-2026-118، الاسم` (cvbooster.ai)
  - Rule: if the ad specifies a format, copy it exactly. Avoid «طلب وظيفة», «CV» or an empty subject.
- Body: 3–5 short lines (name, role, years of experience, top skills, city/availability, phone). Bilingual Arabic + English is common because many HR teams are expat. Attach the CV as a PDF named clearly, e.g. `Name_Title_2026.pdf`.
- Requirements that come up often: transferable Iqama (non-Saudis), Saudi nationality for Saudized roles, gender preference, city.

## Saudization / Nitaqat
- Nitaqat classifies firms by their share of Saudi employees; many professions (sales, admin, HR, accounting, customer service, engineering levels) carry localisation quotas. That makes Saudi candidates especially valuable for SMEs, so the tool highlights nationality and Iqama status in the email.
- Vision 2030 programmes (HRDF Tamheer and wage support) encourage hiring nationals.

## Popular fields in email ads
Construction/MEP and engineering, facility management/O&M technicians, healthcare (nurses, pharmacists), sales and retail, admin/office, accounting, IT and support, logistics.

## Anti-spam and ethics
- Gmail/Workspace bulk-sender rules: keep volume low, use a personal account, never send identical blasts. The tool defaults to user-approved batches and a daily cap of 20, and never emails the same address twice.
- PDPL (Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, enforced since Sept 2024): explicit consent, purpose limitation, the right to delete. Implemented as account and data deletion in settings.
